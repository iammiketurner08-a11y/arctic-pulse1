Patient information

Know what to expect before, during and after your appointment. Clear information is part of good care.

Your visit

Preparing for your first appointment

Bring any information that may help your clinician understand the issue, including relevant medical history, medication changes, previous imaging or reports, and the activities you are trying to return to.

Wear comfortable clothing. Depending on the service, you may be asked to move, perform simple tests or change positions. Your clinician will explain options and obtain consent before assessment or treatment.

Consent and communication

Consent is ongoing. You can ask questions, decline a technique, request a change in positioning or stop an assessment or treatment at any time. We want you to understand the purpose, expected benefit and reasonable alternatives before proceeding.

Appointments and cancellations

Appointment length, fees, cancellation notice and late-arrival policies should be confirmed in the booking system before launch. This website package intentionally leaves business-specific financial terms for the clinic to finalize.

Insurance and receipts

Coverage differs between insurers and plans. Patients are responsible for confirming whether a service or provider is eligible under their benefits. Where applicable, appropriate receipts can be provided for eligible regulated services.

Privacy

Personal and health information should be collected, used and stored only for legitimate care and administrative purposes in accordance with applicable privacy requirements. Before launch, the clinic should insert its finalized privacy policy, retention practices and contact process for privacy questions.

Website terms

Website content is general educational information and should not be treated as individual diagnosis or medical advice. In an emergency or when urgent medical assessment is required, contact the appropriate local emergency service or healthcare facility.
