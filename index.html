About Arctic Pulse

Rooted in the North. Committed to your health.

Arctic Pulse Human Performance & Wellness Ltd. is a multidisciplinary healthcare practice built around clinical reasoning, practical movement and meaningful human connection.

Our aim is to help people feel capable in their bodies—whether they are recovering from injury, managing physical demands, returning to activity or building healthier routines.
