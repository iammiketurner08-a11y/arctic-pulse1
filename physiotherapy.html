Physiotherapy

Assessment, rehabilitation and progressive movement care for injury, mobility and physical function.

Clinical care, clearly explained

Rehabilitation that gives you a pathway back to function.

Physiotherapy focuses on understanding why an activity is limited and what can be changed through education, exercise, load management and, where appropriate, hands-on treatment.

Common questions

Before your first visit

These answers are general guidance. Your clinician will confirm what is appropriate for your individual situation.

Physiotherapy commonly supports musculoskeletal pain, injury rehabilitation, mobility limitations and return-to-activity goals. A clinician will determine whether physiotherapy is appropriate after assessment.

Active rehabilitation is often an important part of physiotherapy because it helps build capacity. Your exercises should be realistic, targeted and scaled to your current level.

Persistent pain can still respond to a structured plan that addresses movement, loading, confidence, recovery and contributing factors. Results vary by person and condition.

If assessment suggests that further medical investigation or another professional is appropriate, your physiotherapist can discuss next steps and referral options.
