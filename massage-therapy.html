What to expect

A practical care pathway built around your goals.

Your plan should make sense. We explain the reasoning, set measurable priorities and adapt care as your response and needs evolve.

Conversation and screening

We review your symptoms, health history, goals and any factors that may change treatment.

Assessment

Movement, tenderness, range, function and relevant clinical findings are considered.

Treatment

A tailored combination of manual techniques and positioning is used based on tolerance and goals.

Plan forward

You leave with clear next steps, which may include movement, recovery strategies or follow-up care.
