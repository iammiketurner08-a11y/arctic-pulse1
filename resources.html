Why recovery is more than rest

Recovery is the process of restoring capacity. Sleep, nutrition, movement, stress, workload and tissue tolerance all influence how quickly a person feels ready to perform again.

Recovery
02

Pain does not always equal damage

Pain is real, but its intensity does not always map directly to tissue damage. Context, sensitivity, stress, sleep and previous experience can influence symptoms. Assessment helps determine what is safe and appropriate.

Pain education
03

The role of progressive loading

Tissues and movement systems adapt when the challenge is appropriate. Progressive loading gradually increases demand so strength, tolerance and confidence can improve without abrupt spikes.

Rehabilitation
04

Preparing for a healthcare appointment

Write down your main concern, how long it has been present, what makes it better or worse, and the activity you most want to return to. Clear priorities help make the visit more useful.
